04 · Prices: what we can reach, when the grid is expensive, and Utah's new day-ahead market¶

Question. Which wholesale electricity prices can we download without a key, for which nodes and years? Does the price peak follow the demand peak or the net-load peak (demand minus wind and solar)? And what changed for Utah's PACE prices inside the sealed test window?

Short answer.

  • Reachable, keyless: CAISO hub and load-point prices from 2023-07-16 (OASIS keeps about 39 months), PACE's 15-minute real-time price as a member of CAISO's western market (WEIM) over the same span, and ERCOT hub prices back to 2010 (downloaded from 2022).
  • Price follows net load, not demand: in every market, the hourly price correlates far better with net load (Spearman ρ 0.53–0.77) than with demand (0.16–0.40). A rush-hour signal built on net load is the better stress proxy.
  • PACE had no day-ahead price before 2026-05-01, when CAISO's day-ahead market (EDAM) went live. Every PACE day-ahead hour is in the sealed window, so here it appears only as counts. The test-window price channel must be chosen before the seal opens.

How to read this notebook. Prices are observed ($/MWh, hourly; real-time is the mean of the 15-minute intervals in each hour). Dollar figures elsewhere are labelled "wholesale energy-cost scenario", never a bill. Statistics and figures come from lab/scripts/recon_prices_profile.py, behind docs/data-profile/workloads-prices.md §2.

Seal. Every statistic and figure uses data before 2025-11-01Z only (drop_sealed, applied inside the recon's series_frame and demand_frame). The sealed window, including all of PACE's EDAM day-ahead prices, appears in §2 and §6 as row counts, missing hours and first/last hours only.

tables_all = rp.load_all_hourly(None)  # includes the sealed window: used for counts only, then dropped


def completeness(tables: dict) -> pl.DataFrame:
    # Rows, expected hours and missing hours per node and window. Counts and first/last hours only.
    rows = []
    for (iso, market), d in tables.items():
        for node in sorted(d["node"].unique().to_list()):
            g = d.filter(pl.col("node") == node)
            first = g["ts_utc"].min()
            for window, a, b in (("pre-validation", first, VALIDATION_START), ("validation", VALIDATION_START, TEST_START),
                                 ("sealed (to 2026-10-25)", TEST_START, CUTOFF)):
                a = max(a, first)
                if b <= a:
                    rows.append({"iso": iso, "market": market, "node": node, "window": window, "rows": 0,
                                 "missing_hours": 0, "first_hour": None, "last_hour": None})
                    continue
                c = P.completeness(g, a, b)
                n = int(c["rows"][0]) if c.height else 0
                rows.append({"iso": iso, "market": market, "node": node, "window": window, "rows": n,
                             "missing_hours": int((b - a).total_seconds() // 3600) - n,
                             "first_hour": f"{c['first'][0]:%Y-%m-%d %H:%M}Z" if n else None,
                             "last_hour": f"{c['last'][0]:%Y-%m-%d %H:%M}Z" if n else None})
    return pl.DataFrame(rows)

Consequences, pre-registered before the seal opens.

  1. PACE's price channel is trained and validated on the WEIM real-time price (ELAP_PACE-APND, 15-min, hourly mean), the only PACE price with history.
  2. In the test window, the PACE price is either real-time throughout (consistent) or EDAM day-ahead from 2026-05-01 (realistic, but a regime change mid-test). The choice is recorded before unsealing, and the two regimes are never stitched into one series (decisions D-017, D-037).
  3. Prices are a Should: dollar results are "wholesale energy-cost scenarios", reported next to the emissions result, never weighted into it.

Decisions this supports¶

From the decision log:

  • D-037: prices are a Should, labelled "wholesale scenario"; PACE trains on WEIM real-time; the test-window PACE channel is pre-registered; the stress proxy is built on net load (§1–§6).
  • D-017: dollar figures are wholesale energy-cost scenarios, and PACE's EDAM regime stays separate from its real-time history (§6).
  • D-012: the stress metric is a "net-load stress proxy", checked against prices (§5).
